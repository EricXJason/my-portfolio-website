跑酷遊戲的創新策略：行動遊戲市場的案例

摘要

隨著智慧型手機的普及,手機遊戲市場如雨後春筍般迅速崛起。跑酷類遊戲作為早期最受
玩家歡迎的遊戲類型之一,一度風靡全球。然而,隨著市場逐漸成熟與遊戲類型的多元化,跑
酷遊戲因創新停滯而逐漸失去優勢,玩家興趣減退,開發者也將重心轉向更具商業價值的遊戲
品類,導致跑酷遊戲在市場上逐步衰退。
本文通過文獻分析和案例研究,詳細探討了市面上多款跑酷遊戲的設計和盈利元素。這些
設計包括但不限於:引入多層次關卡設計以增加遊戲的深度和挑戰性;結合隨機生成元素以提
升遊戲的可重玩性;引入角色成長系統以增加玩家的投入感和遊戲黏性;以及探索新的美術風
格和敘事手法以提升遊戲的整體吸引力。最終,基於研究結果,本文提出了跑酷遊戲未來可能
的創新方向,並提供具體的改進建議,為未來的遊戲設計提供更具實證性的建議。

關鍵字:行動遊戲、手機遊戲、跑酷遊戲、遊戲設計、盈利機制

Abstract

With the widespread adoption of smartphones, the mobile gaming market has risen rapidly, akin to
a flourishing industry. Among the early game genres most beloved by players, runner games once
enjoyed global popularity. However, as the market matured and game genres diversified, the stagnation
of innovation in runner games led to their decline. Player interest waned, and developers shifted their
focus to more commercially lucrative game genres, causing runner games to gradually fade from the
market.

This paper employs literature analysis and case studies to explore in detail the design and
monetization elements of various runner games on the market. These designs include, but are not limited
to: introducing multi-layered level designs to enhance game depth and challenge; incorporating
procedurally generated elements to improve replayability; implementing character progression systems
to increase player engagement and retention; and experimenting with new art styles and narrative
techniques to enhance overall game appeal. Based on the research findings, this paper proposes potential
innovative directions for runner games and provides concrete recommendations for improvement,
offering evidence-based insights for future game design.

Keywords: mobile gaming, smartphone games, runner games, game design, monetization strategies

《覺醒協議》：基於認知發展理論的虛擬實境遊戲設計

摘要

本研究採實踐本位研究取向,以 Piaget 之認知發展四階段作為設計架構,將
感知探索、符號表徵、具體因果與抽象整合等心智特徵,轉譯為虛擬實境作品《覺
醒協議》中遞進式之場景關卡。本創作旨在將虛擬生命由「受控規訓」邁向「自主
意識覺醒」的心理轉變進行結構化呈現,使主體心智之建構奠基於體驗者的身體化
行動。
全案秉持身體化認知之觀點,運用虛擬實境的第一人稱感官封閉性與即時互
動反饋,建構一套探討意識生成之交互機制。最終,本研究釐清了跨領域理論轉譯
在藝術實踐中的邊界與限制,並提煉出從理論框架走向反思主體性生成之「去模型
化」創作洞見。此一反思歷程可作為後續跨領域之新媒體交互敘事與實踐本位研究
之實踐參照。

關鍵詞:虛擬實境、認知發展、意識覺醒、身體化認知、互動敘事

Abstract

This study adopts a practice-based research approach, utilizing Piaget's four stages
of cognitive development as a design framework. It translates cognitive characteristics—
sensorimotor exploration, symbolic representation, concrete causality, and abstract
integration—into progressive interactive levels within the virtual reality artwork
“Awakening Protocol”. The project aims to structure the psychological transition of an
artificial lifeform from "controlled discipline" to "autonomous awakening," grounding
the construction of the subjective mind in the experiencer's embodied actions.
This creative practice leverages virtual reality's first-person sensory isolation and
real-time interactive feedback to construct an interactive mechanism for exploring
consciousness generation. Ultimately, this research clarifies the boundaries and
constraints of cross-disciplinary theoretical translation within artistic practice. It distills
creative insights that transcend rigid modeling, shifting from an adherence to theoretical
frameworks toward a critical reflection on the generation of subjectivity. This reflective
process serves as a practice-based reference for future cross-disciplinary interactive
narratives and practice-based research.

Keywords: Virtual Reality, Cognitive Development, Consciousness Awakening,
Embodied Cognition, Interactive Narrative
